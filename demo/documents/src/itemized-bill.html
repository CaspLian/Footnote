About these charges

Rev code is the hospital department that provided the item or service, such as 0301 for chemistry lab tests or 0450 for the emergency department. CPT/HCPCS identifies the specific service, drug or supply. Insurance adjustments are the amounts your insurer’s contract does not allow the hospital to collect. Compare this statement with the Explanation of Benefits from your insurer; if they do not match, call us at {{hosp_phone_billing}} before you pay. Financial assistance: plain language summary

Linnet Valley Medical Center provides free or discounted emergency and other medically necessary care to patients who qualify under our Financial Assistance Policy. You can apply whether or not you have insurance.

Household income, as a percent of the Federal Poverty GuidelinesDiscount on your balance
Up to 200%100% (free care)
201% to 300%75%
301% to 400%50%

Patients who qualify will not be charged more for emergency or other medically necessary care than the amounts generally billed to people who have insurance covering that care.

How to apply

  • Online at {{hosp_financial_assistance_url}}, or by phone at {{hosp_phone_financial_assistance}}.
  • In person at Financial Counseling, Main Lobby, {{hosp_street}}, {{hosp_city}}.
  • By mail: Financial Counseling, {{hosp_correspondence_po_box}}, {{hosp_city}}, {{hosp_state}} {{hosp_correspondence_zip4}}.

Include proof of household income, such as recent pay stubs or a tax return. You may apply up to 240 days after the date of your first statement for this visit. We will not send your account to a collection agency while your application is being reviewed. Copies of the policy, the application and this summary are free, and are available in Spanish and other languages.

Questions about your bill

Call {{hosp_department}} at {{hosp_phone_billing}} (TTY {{hosp_tty}}), {{hosp_billing_hours}}. Please have your guarantor account number ready.

If you think a charge is wrong

Call us, or write to Billing Review, {{hosp_department}}, {{hosp_correspondence_po_box}}, {{hosp_city}}, {{hosp_state}} {{hosp_correspondence_zip4}}. Include your guarantor account number, the date of service, the charge you are questioning and the reason. We will place your account on hold while we review it.

Payment plans

If you cannot pay the full balance by the due date, call us to set up an interest-free monthly payment plan. Balances with no payment or payment plan may be placed with a collection agency, but not until at least 120 days after your first statement.

Insurance

We bill your insurance for you. Your insurer sends you an Explanation of Benefits (EOB) that shows what it paid and what you may owe. The balance on this statement should match the amount your EOB lists as your responsibility.

Protection from surprise medical bills

Federal law protects you from surprise bills (balance billing) for emergency care and for care from out-of-network providers at an in-network hospital: you should not be charged more than your plan’s copayment, coinsurance and deductible. Wrongly billed? Call {{hosp_phone_billing}}. Full notice: {{hosp_surprise_billing_url}}.

Notice of nondiscrimination

Linnet Valley Medical Center complies with applicable Federal civil rights laws and does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, national origin, age, disability, or sex. We provide free aids and services to people with disabilities to communicate effectively with us, such as qualified sign language interpreters and written information in other formats, and free language services to people whose primary language is not English, such as qualified interpreters and information written in other languages. To request these services, call {{hosp_phone_billing}} (TTY {{hosp_tty}}). If you believe we have failed to provide these services or discriminated in another way, you can file a grievance with our Civil Rights Coordinator at {{hosp_phone_civil_rights}}, or by mail at {{hosp_correspondence_po_box}}, {{hosp_city}}, {{hosp_state}} {{hosp_correspondence_zip4}}. You can also file a civil rights complaint with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, Office for Civil Rights.
