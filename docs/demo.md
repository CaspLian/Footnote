# Footnote review walkthrough

1. Finish personal setup and choose your working language.
2. Add a bill or explanation of benefits from the camera, photos or PDF files.
3. Confirm the selected pages and document types before sending them for document reading.
4. Compare the extracted details with the originals. Correct unclear amounts and leave unknown values blank.
5. Open the review. Medicare references and sourced findings provide questions to ask, rather than guaranteed savings.
6. Listen to the saved explanation or discuss the saved bill with the voice helper when the chosen language is supported.
7. Prepare a billing request or insurer appeal, edit it, and copy or export it. Send it yourself.
8. Mark submission only after taking that action. Record a follow-up date and reopen the case from Bills or Timeline.

Preview voices under Settings → Agent voice. Previews use short English clips; document readings and conversations use the chosen working language where supported. Android playback requires verification on the installed development client; automated and web results are recorded separately in `PROJECT_STATUS.md`.

For offline document imports, enter and confirm the details manually. A missing connection is an explicit unavailable state. App lock controls access and does not encrypt imported files. Follow-up records do not schedule system notifications or determine legal deadlines.
